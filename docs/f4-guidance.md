## Research, 18 November 2026

- [GOV.UK display rules](https://www.gov.uk/displaying-number-plates/rules-number-plates) describe plate faces and markings, the post-September 2021 standard, raised lettering and motorcycle dates/layouts.
- [DVLA INF104](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/vehicle-registration-numbers-and-number-plates) sections 3, 4 and 7 cover registration formats, misrepresentation and the specification for spacing and character shapes. Short and angled outlines need checking against the actual characters and margins.
- [Flag guidance](https://www.gov.uk/displaying-number-plates/flags-identifiers-and-stickers) lists permitted domestic identifiers and separately addresses travel abroad. The EU option is a display-only catalogue choice.
- [Getting number plates made up](https://www.gov.uk/displaying-number-plates/getting-number-plates-made-up) requires a registered supplier to inspect original proof of identity/address and entitlement. A licence and V5C are examples; original document handling needs supplier confirmation before live commerce.
