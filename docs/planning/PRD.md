# Product requirements
Current implementation uses F1–F6 with B1 and A1 as described above. Audience: UK drivers choosing a physical replacement plate for a registration they are entitled to use. The customer's decisions are finish, shape, side, fit and total price. The operator's decision is whether a paid order is ready to make.
* Publish all supplier-approved road styles. Each product has a linkable description, rendered example, fit constraints, price and ordering path. Special formats can enter manual review. Use UK spelling and GBP.
* Display permitted formatting feedback while configuring. An unknown format routes to help. Validate the exact dimensions, spacing, identifiers, materials, markings and permitted combinations against the approved catalogue before sale. A registration text match cannot certify a finished product. See [GOV.UK display rules](https://www.gov.uk/displaying-number-plates/rules-number-plates).
* Before payment explain evidence, delivery and cancellation terms. After payment provide upload and resume links. Staff own verification and release. Missing evidence stays on hold for a staff call.
* Launch includes mobile storefront, admin, Stripe, secure uploads and review collection, product pages and initial buying guides. Logo work and live lookup are draft add-ons. Obtain scope and API access confirmation for live lookup. The proposed chatbot, lead automation and ongoing SEO are separate workstreams.
* Measure studio-to-checkout rate, paid orders, upload completion, wait to verification, dispatch and genuine review submissions. Count eBay clicks and WhatsApp clicks separately from confirmed orders and enquiries.
Acceptance: a buyer can choose any approved combination and see the same item, registration and total through payment and fulfilment. A staff member can resolve a missing-document order without contacting a developer. Handoff: Website Flow.md.
