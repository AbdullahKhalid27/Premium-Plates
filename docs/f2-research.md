## Palette and product framing

- Keep beige `#F0EDE6` across the masthead and supporting page, with the live plate framed in Kiln plum `#4A1F39`. Use apricot `#FF9876` for focus and selected controls on plum. Calculated flat-swatch contrast is 11.62:1 for beige against plum, 6.47:1 for apricot against plum and 1.80:1 for apricot against beige. Use plum or dark ink for functional details on beige and check rendered gradients and focus states at the F3 checkpoint. The F1 stylesheet already supplies all three tokens, Georgia for display type, and the layout loads the existing sans and plate font. [Design decisions](planning/DESIGN.md) · [F1 tokens](../premium-plates-site/src/app/foundation.css) · [Layout](../premium-plates-site/src/app/layout.tsx)
- Kiln frames the plate in an arch and stacks the controls at narrow widths. Transfer Bugatti's product-first use of space, BMW M's restraint with accent colour and Wired's editorial type hierarchy. The styling comes from the project palette and typography. ## Controls and MVP reuse

- Kiln's native range adjusts both the plate angle and highlight position. A separate headlights button toggles illumination and exposes its pressed state. Side and finish buttons update the same preview and caption while retaining registration, angle and light. Bring over the native slider and button affordances so touch and keyboard users can operate them directly. The MVP detail component's pointer tilt handles mouse and pen. Keep that interaction available for inspection while letting explicit controls determine a repeatable angle and light. [Kiln markup](../../Playground/kiln.html) · [Kiln logic](../../Playground/studio.js) · [MVP interactive plate](../premium-plates-site/src/components/InteractivePlate.tsx)
- `StudioPage` already normalizes and shares registration, finish and order side with the configurator. Reuse its registration handler, `Plate` and `selectKind`, mapping Flat to `regular`. The existing order side includes a pair and affects pricing. Keep front/rear viewing as preview state so a customer can inspect both faces of a pair without changing the configured quantity. Preserve existing on-car fitment, finish comparison and neutral placeholder identity. Keep registration in client state and describe finish visuals as illustrative pending physical-sample approval. At the F3 checkpoint, verify touch and keyboard operation, retained registration and selections, reduced motion, both faces under each light, and first-load performance on representative Android hardware.
