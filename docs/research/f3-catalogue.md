### EU identifier

Reviewed 18 November 2026. Exclude the EU symbol from new and replacement road plate configurations. Regulation 4 preserves the EU symbol provision for plates or devices originally fixed to the vehicle and compliant before the end of the UK–EU transition period. GOV.UK lists EU flags in its travel guidance because vehicles can still carry those earlier plates. Drivers with an EU flag on their plate generally need a separate UK sticker abroad. Spain, Cyprus and Malta require a sticker regardless of plate markings, and Ireland does not require one. If offered in the display-only preview, the EU badge remains a display option when selecting, restoring or adding the configuration to the basket. [Amending regulations, regulations 1 and 4](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2020/1363/pdfs/uksi_20201363_en.pdf) · [GOV.UK flags, identifiers and stickers](https://www.gov.uk/displaying-number-plates/flags-identifiers-and-stickers)
