# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

UK car owners who care about the visual details of their cars and want to compare number plate finishes before deciding what suits the vehicle.

## Product Purpose

Premium Plates is a frontend MVP for exploring and personalising UK number plates. A visitor can enter a registration, compare finishes, view it on a plate and a car scene, and make a demo basket selection. The current success criterion is a compelling, usable client demonstration.

## Operating Context

People arrive on a marketing page, inspect styles, compare finish and fit, then customise a registration. The product categories to cover are Regular, 3D Gel, 4D, Short, Hex, Show, and Motorbike plates. The supplied night-drive clips are presentation media, not a legal product specification.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Frontend only: no database, backend, payment processing, document collection, or live order submission.
- Next.js App Router, TypeScript, and Tailwind CSS.
- Responsive desktop and mobile design, keyboard access, and reduced-motion support.
- MVP prices are illustrative until the client confirms a price sheet. Do not present them as verified competitor prices.
- Claims about road legality, dispatch, warranty, reviews, and materials need client confirmation before use as business facts.
- The three supplied videos should become one seven-second loop focused on the plate.

## Brand Commitments

- The MVP uses the name Premium Plates, chosen by the user.
- A luxury automotive feeling with a classy, uncommon palette; avoid purple and generic template styling.
- Motion and hover feedback should serve the shopping experience. The user explicitly wants a distinctive, non-static interface.

## Evidence on Hand

- User-supplied videos: `../1st.mp4`, `../2nd part.mp4`, `../3rd part.mp4`.
- Stitch export at `../stitch-export/` as an anti-reference for density and generic card patterns.
- Competitor site `https://www.premiumplates.com/` as a category and feature reference; its testimonials and commercial claims are not ours to copy.

## Product Principles

1. Let the plate and the vehicle show the difference between finishes.
2. Make exploration and customisation obvious within the first viewport.
3. Give each section a distinct purpose and visual rhythm.
4. Be explicit when an MVP value is illustrative or a control is a demo.

## Open Decisions

- Whether the black-and-gold plate in the supplied video should be labelled as show-plate footage while the configurator displays road-style plates.
- Whether the final product will prioritise direct checkout or quote requests; this MVP can preview both paths without submitting data.
